Inclined Venturi Meter for Fertilizer Irrigation

Used SolidWorks and COMSOL in a four-person class project to design and analyze an irrigation-inspired Venturi meter operating at a 45° incline; compared simulations and theory with physical prototype measurements.

More detail
  • Geometry: 160 mm length, 18 mm inlet/outlet, 6 mm throat, 135° elbow, and angled manometer bases so the tubes stay vertical on the incline; the team used a resin-printed prototype with a target budget below $25.
